# Ayunilayam - Meru Sansthapana Chikitsa™ Carousel

A premium 10-slide Instagram carousel (1080 × 1350 px, 4:5 aspect ratio) for **Ayunilayam - Kerala Ayurveda & Panchakarma**, highlighting the **Meru Sansthapana Chikitsa™** Spine Restoration Program.

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

- **1080 × 1350 px Resolution**: Pixel-perfect dimensions optimized for Instagram and social media carousels.
- **Fixed Logo Cropping**: Prominently displayed and uncropped footer logos on Cover & Closing slides, with a clean wave footer on process slides (Slides 2–9).
- **Expanded Real Photographs**: Enlarged clinical and treatment photographs for maximum visual impact without AI distortion or stretching.
- **Concise & Non-Repetitive Copy**: Highlighting key clinical steps, treatment benefits, and recovery precautions in punchy, easy-to-read text.
- **Authentic Brand Design System**:
  - **Cream Background**: `#F8F3EA`
  - **Dark Olive Banner & Accents**: `#3D5239`
  - **Deep Sage**: `#4A5548`
  - **Gold Inset Frame**: `#C29B53`
  - **Serif Headings**: Playfair Display & Cormorant Garamond
